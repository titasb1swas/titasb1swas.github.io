Privacy Policy for Golpo Sandhan

Last Updated: September 2026

Golpo Sandhan ("we," "our," or "us") provides a mobile application designed as an index, curation tool, and search discovery platform for Bengali audio literature. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application (com.golposandhan.story) handles information, respects user privacy, and interfaces with third-party platforms.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require user accounts, registrations, phone numbers, email addresses, names, or payment details to use the application.
  • Media Hosting: Golpo Sandhan does not host, store, cache, upload, re-transmit, or distribute any copyrighted audio, video, or media files on its servers. All metadata (such as titles, author names, character tags, and summaries) is indexed to help users discover publicly available content.

2. On-Device Storage & Local Data

  • Local Preferences: Your saved favorites, filter choices, search terms, and listening history are stored locally on your device (using on-device database storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external private server.
  • Device Telemetry: Standard operating system identifiers and general network state may be processed ephemerally on-device to ensure proper image loading, caching, and catalog delta synchronization.

3. Playback Architecture & Third-Party Services

Golpo Sandhan facilitates access to publicly hosted media solely through standard external links and native web rendering:

  • External Application Redirection:
    • "Listen on YouTube" & "Listen on YT Music": Directs the user to the official YouTube or YouTube Music application installed on their device.
    • "Listen on External App" & "Copy Link": Opens the official video URL in the user's preferred external web browser or system application.
  • In-App Foreground Web Player:
    • "Listen Now": Loads the official YouTube web player directly from YouTube’s servers using a standard in-app browser view. Playback occurs solely in the active foreground in accordance with third-party service terms and does not play in the background when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.
    • Interaction with this media is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. All views, ad revenues, and engagement metrics directly benefit the original content creators and channel uploaders.
  • Google AdMob: We display banner advertisements on catalog, metadata, and search navigation screens to support server maintenance and app development costs. We do not inject ads into, overlay ads upon, or monetize third-party audio/video streams. AdMob may process device advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Cloud Infrastructure (Firebase): Used exclusively for synchronizing public catalog metadata (episode lists, series, writers, and genre tags) to your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

Golpo Sandhan is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

5. Intellectual Property & DMCA Takedown Policy

Golpo Sandhan respects intellectual property rights. All story scripts, audio recordings, musical arrangements, and associated visual artwork belong strictly to their respective authors, voice artists, producers, and official YouTube channel uploaders.

If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and wish to request removal of an indexed entry from our search catalog, send a notice with:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • The specific story title or video URL indexed in our app.
  • Your contact information (name, organization, and email address).
