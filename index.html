The Smart Directory for Bengali Audio Literature

Tired of endless scrolling on YouTube? Golpo Sandhan organizes Bengal's richest audio stories into an instant, searchable catalog.

Multi-Field Search & Filter

Filter effortlessly across Writers, Character Series (Feluda, Byomkesh, Shonku), Production Shows, and Episode Duration to find the exact listen for your mood.

Series & Multi-Part Tracking

Multi-part sagas are grouped systematically. Never lose track of which episode comes next in complex detective or horror sagas.

Detailed Literary Summaries

Preview authentic Bengali synopses, publication years, ratings, and view counts before diving into an episode.
